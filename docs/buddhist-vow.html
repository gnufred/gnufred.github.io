My Buddhist vow

Taking refuge in the Three Jewels and rebuilding one habit at a time.

For my 43rd birthday, I offer this vow to myself and to others. I vow to work toward enlightenment and to help liberate others from suffering.

The story

I was born into the Christian tradition, and my heart always followed the path of the Buddha. I found guidance in the teachings of His Holiness the Dalai Lama and Zen Master Thich Nhat Hanh.

Five years ago, I was at my lowest. I was burned out, chronically in pain, unable to sleep, drowning in panic, and quietly planning to leave this world. I was lost.

I had a son. And a daughter on the way.

In one desperate moment of clarity, I made a decision. From the suffering, I would transform.

Books helped me find a place to start. I began practicing Tummo breathing meditations and doing short sessions of push-ups, squats, and cardio. Just a few minutes, three times a day. Then I started running once a week.

Slowly, patiently, habit by habit, I built a life around movement, breath, discipline, and simplicity. Today, I can run 12 km on trails in under an hour. I feel stronger, healthier, and happier, and I’m more present in my life.

I wrote more about how I built those daily habits in My health hacks.

Taking refuge

With this vow, I formally take refuge in the Three Jewels. I’ve chosen the Dharma name Rishiśūra, Sage-Warrior. This is the foundation I carry for the rest of my life.

If you’re struggling, start small, start now, and keep walking. Let one good habit take root before you add another.
